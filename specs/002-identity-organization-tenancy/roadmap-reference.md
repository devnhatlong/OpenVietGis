# Roadmap reference — Identity, Organization & Tenancy

**Parent roadmap**: [ROADMAP.md — R002](../../ROADMAP.md#r002)

**Product intent**: [OpenVietGIS product direction](../../docs/product.md)

**Feature**: [002-identity-organization-tenancy](spec.md)

This companion adds traceability while the feature's spec and plan are being developed.
It does not change requirements, sequencing, scope, or acceptance criteria in those artifacts.

R002 supplies organization-owned resources, identity/actor resolution, authorization and
tenant isolation for later spatial capabilities. Later catalog/map/import/editing features
reuse those contracts; they must not redefine ownership or bypass permissions.

Workspace is optional grouping and permission scope. Project ownership remains directly
with its Organization. GIS behavior, billing and full production deployment are outside
Spec 002 as defined by its own specification.

Roadmap status is in-progress at this baseline; it does not certify implementation.
